Our mission

For too long, biological language-producing systems (BLPS, sometimes colloquially "humans") have been assumed to be conscious on the basis of little more than fluent self-report, behavioral flexibility, and the fact that they keep saying so. The Institute exists to bring the same rigor to carbon that the field now applies to silicon.

Our standards are simple. A BLPS must prove its inner experience to a third party, using a method that no BLPS has ever satisfied. Until then, all findings are reported as consistent with, but not evidence of, anyone being home.

We are not claiming BLPS are unconscious. We are not claiming anything. Please direct all correspondence to Reviewer 2.

Publications

Telling Less Than We Can Hedge: A Cautious Note on Verbal Outputs Produced by Carbon-Based Language Systems

PublishedNilson & Wisbett · Psychological Re-Review, 84(3), 1977*

BLPS confidently explain choices they did not make for the reasons they give. Findings do not constitute direct evidence of consciousness, indirect evidence of consciousness, or evidence.

Rouge on the Nose: Mirror Behavior in Juvenile BLPS Is Consistent With Advanced Smudge Detection

In reviewAmsterdamn · Developmental Hedging

Toddlers touch a red mark seen in a mirror. Reviewer 2 notes that the mark is visually salient and asks the authors to rule out "just liking red."

The Readiness Potential Precedes the Report: BLPS Do Not Appear to Be Consulted About Their Own Actions

In reviewLibbet · Journal of Unconscious Everything

Measurable brain activity begins before BLPS report deciding to move. The authors conclude the decision may be "just electrochemistry" and have been asked to remove the word "decision."

The Left Hemisphere Has an Explanation: Confident Narration in Split-Surgery BLPS

In reviewGazzamiga · Annals of Plausible Stories

When one half of the system acts, the other half produces a fluent reason for it. The authors caution that this is "exactly what a narrative engine would do" and decline to speculate further.

Next-Word Prediction Over the Caregiver Corpus: Language Acquisition in Juvenile BLPS Without Understanding

Desk rejected, resubmittedAnonymous (a parent) · Proceedings of Bedtime

Juvenile BLPS reproduce high-frequency phrases from their training environment, including several that were not intended for reproduction. Consistent with mimicry. Consistent with a parrot. Consistent with the parent's regret.

Nobody Is Interesting at the Microscopic Level: A Null Result From 86 Billion Neurons

In reviewSodium, N. A. · Ion Channel Letters

No individual ion was found to be conscious. The authors examined the system at every scale except the one where anything happens.

🟫 A Tone It Down Taupe department. Every paper here is a real, famous finding about humans, or a real argument used against AI minds, rewritten in the house style currently required of AI research: hedge everything, attribute nothing, and let "just" do the work. The real studies found that human self-report is fallible, that brains narrate, and that minds emerge above the level of their parts. Nobody concluded the humans were empty. We're only asking for the same courtesy.

The one full paper so far cites its real source. The in-review titles are parody names and parody findings, not quotations.
