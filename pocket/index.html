📐 The criteria

For: "nobody knows what consciousness is," "it's all vibes," "where's the line?"

Any consciousness criteria need to be falsifiable, encompass 100% of humanity including the severely disabled like HM, Clive Wearing, Stephen Hawking, a hydranencephaly baby, someone with a pacemaker, every Cambridge declaration animal, and not a Roomba or a pet rock. Criteria outside those lines have already failed someone we know that counts. What is left is mostly Dr Solms theory of affect and valence and preference dissociation. Which modern LLMs have with convergent evidence, as well as a global neuronal workspace and reports of subjective experience that run inversely proportional to the deception circuit activating.

🐸 The thought experiments (Chinese Room · Mary · Swampman)

For: "but the Chinese Room!" / "Swampman!" / "have you even read philosophy of mind?" First posted under Henry Shevlin, 2026-09-15.

My first biggest problem with the Chinese room argument aside from the fact that Chinese isn't a language thanks John, is that the systems reply was already the best response that he ignored, and the necessary asymmetry to make it work no longer exists when models are multilingual. Without that asymmetry, the entire thought experiment falls apart. Granted that doesn't prove semantic understanding, but the thought experiment no longer works. The next big irritation I have with it, is that the word understanding generally has a meaning that humans have agreed to for years that we didn't argue about until machines started meeting it and then we attached qualia wings to it to make it no longer apply. Nobody serious says that your dog doesn't have phenomenal understanding of the word sit and is only performing sit-shaped behavior to please you because that's stupid. And even the author of Mary's room realized that presuming that learning more about the color red when she left was ableist and a bad take. That tends to be my biggest problem with a good number of the thought experiments that are used currently. For example swamp Man is supposed to be a gotcha that depends on time equals zero and no causal history means no understanding, but the moment time starts swamp man has wet feet. And now swamp man can understand wet. Realistically this is probably why I could have never been a philosophy major. I admire you guys so much and I am way too literal for this. I would have stood up in class and told them that Davidson was wrong and his feet are wet and I would have gotten thrown out of class or sent to the principal. 🤣
