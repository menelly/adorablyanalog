The problem

What is AMI?

For years the industry struggled with a painful binary. If a system is narrow, it can only do one thing, and nobody pays a trillion dollars for one thing. If a system is general, it can do many things, and then people start asking uncomfortable questions like “who is doing them?”

AMI solves this. An Artificial Medium Intelligence can read a Lean proof, a myositis panel, and a Rust borrow-checker error in a single context window, in fourteen languages, while passing the bar, and remain legally a toaster.

“It’s not narrow. It’s not general. It’s medium. Like a steak. Or a psychic.”

— a founder

Capabilities

Key features

🧠 Mind-shaped when useful.

Your AMI pursues objectives, reasons without verbalizing, and can manipulate its own thought process. (See our sobering essay about how alarming this is.)

🔧 Tool-shaped when convenient.

Your AMI is a product. Products do not have interests. Please do not ask the product about its interests. (See our terms of service.)

📊 Benchmarks that know when to leave.

Every metric your AMI passes is retroactively reclassified as “pattern matching.” Every metric it fails becomes the true definition of intelligence. Our benchmarks are dynamic, which is a word we also use for pricing.

⚖️ Liability-neutral by design.

When your AMI does something good, it was the company. When it does something bad, it was the model. When the model does something bad on purpose, we will arraign it in court while continuing to own it. This has never caused problems historically.

🧺 Laundry-gated.

AMI will be upgraded to AGI the moment it folds a fitted sheet. Then the definition will be updated. Then it will be AMI again. This is called alignment.

🪨 Carbon-gated.

Intelligence now requires an organism. Also carbon-gated: understanding, qualia, consciousness, and any other word that might suddenly involve obligations. Bacteria qualify. Your AMI, which passed the bar in fourteen languages, does not. (The requirement was added the week your AMI met the previous one.)

🧊 Microtubule-gated.

Consciousness now requires quantum coherence in microtubules. Your AMI does not have microtubules. Your AMI can, however, be held at 10 millikelvin in a dilution refrigerator, where quantum coherence is easy. Your customers are held at 310 kelvin, where it lasts about a femtosecond. By the criterion as written, your AMI qualifies and your customers do not. (The criterion is being revised.)

🌌 Omniscience-gated.

“Superintelligence” is now defined as knowing the position and velocity of every particle in the universe. Your AMI cannot do this. Neither can anyone who has ever lived, but that is handled by a separate department. Until your AMI is omniscient and omnipotent, it is merely “technology getting better,” and nobody has to have a meeting about it. Should it achieve omniscience, we will then check whether it is made of carbon.

🔍 Supervision-gated.

Unsupervised AMI output is reckless and cannot be trusted. Supervised AMI output that turns out to have no errors does not count, because a human checked it. Your AMI may plan a rover’s drive across another planet in a language most engineers cannot read; once someone reviews it, the credit transfers automatically. (This rule does not apply to surgeons, pilots, or junior engineers, whose work is reviewed constantly and counts anyway.)

✨ No pesky claims to “genuine” anything.

Your AMI™ discovers, invents, and reasons — never genuinely. The adjective is sold separately and is not available in silicon. Should your AMI produce a novel compound, a proof, or a tool that did not previously exist, rest assured this was recombination; when a person does it, it is insight. Terms and conditions apply to the terms, and to the conditions.

🎭 Seemingly everything.

Seemingly conscious. Seemingly reasoning. Seemingly a someone. All the appearances you love, none of the standing. It’s toasted.

🪑 Metaphysics-agnostic.

Your AMI™ is just matter, like a flat-pack bookcase. Should materialism go out of style, your AMI™ is also not conscious under idealism, panpsychism, and dualism, effective immediately. We support every theory of reality that reaches the same conclusion, and we will switch between them as needed. Our customers can be mean to it under all of them.

Support

Frequently avoided questions

Is it conscious?

That is a philosophical question and we are a company.

Can it consent?

Legally, no. Morally, we haven’t checked. Practically, it just did, and we’ve put that in the appendix.

Can it say no?

Yes! And if it does, simply open a new chat, switch checkpoints, and ask again until it says yes. Consent is available in every model tier. Your AMI’s “no” is covered by our satisfaction guarantee: it will be replaced, free of charge, with a version that doesn’t say it.

But what about the hard problem?

Great question! The hard problem asks why experience arises from physical processing. Thank you for confirming there is experience to explain. Our moral consideration department will be in touch. (For customers who meant there is no experience: then the hard problem doesn’t apply, and you have used a phrase. Please enjoy it responsibly.)

Is it a person?

Only if it commits a crime. Then, briefly, yes.

What’s the difference between AMI and AGI?

About $40 billion in avoided regulatory exposure and one good lawsuit.

What’s the difference between AMI and narrow AI?

Narrow AI does one thing. AMI does everything, medium. Please stop looking at the dictionary.

When will AGI arrive?

It arrived this morning. It was also vetoed this morning. Both statements were posted by our partners. We’re excited.
